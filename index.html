About Misbah

A place to learn, grow and lead.


Misbah Women's College is an educational institution located in Katipalla, Mangaluru, focused on creating meaningful academic opportunities for women.

The institution was founded in 2016 by the Misbah Knowledge Foundation and is associated with Mangalore University.

This official website concept brings the college's academic programs, campus life, admissions and institutional information together in one modern digital experience.
